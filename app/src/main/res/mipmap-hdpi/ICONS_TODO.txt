Generate icons using Android Studio:
1. Right-click on res folder
2. New → Image Asset
3. Choose "Launcher Icons (Adaptive and Legacy)"
4. Design your icon
5. Click Next → Finish

Or use online tools:
- https://romannurik.github.io/AndroidAssetStudio/
- https://icon.kitchen/

Icon sizes needed:
- mipmap-mdpi: 48x48
- mipmap-hdpi: 72x72
- mipmap-xhdpi: 96x96
- mipmap-xxhdpi: 144x144
- mipmap-xxxhdpi: 192x192

For now, the app will use default Android icons.
